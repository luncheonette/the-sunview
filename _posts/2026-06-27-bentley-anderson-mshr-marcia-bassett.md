BENTLEY ANDERSON -a New York City artist working in the realm of noise and
experimental music. His focus is on a texture-based aesthetic that attempts a
narrative expression with sound.

MSHR initiates “Negative Flower,” A new system for live improvisation with
virtual acoustic feedback and sculptural patterning. Wind is blowing through a
garden of stones.

GIRILAL BAARS (SE) / MARCIA BASSETT / CHUCK BETTIS live improvisations with
modular, analog synth and voice, exploring techniques with no formal names.

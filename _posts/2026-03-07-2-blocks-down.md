**Marija Kovačević & Rob Mayson:** The first live encounter between Marija
Kovačević’s Music For Broken Violins and Rob Mayson’s Drum Stool. Decomposed
acoustics; wheezing, scraping drum throne and a pile of violins.

**Marcia Bassett & Taralie Peterson:** In collaboration, Bassett and Peterson
will bring together an organic, playful Alto Sax/Buchla conversation. Delighting
in the shared language of each instrument, through their diverse textures,
chasing the differences in their sounds towards finding a common language of joy
and surprise.

**Keith Fullerton Whitman:** Live electronic music, minimalism, and research
into generative & process/systems music in both the analog & digital domains.
An extensive discography includes releases on kranky, Planet-µ, Nakid, Editions
Mego, PAN, and countless others.

**Pubic Erotic:** Public Erotic is a fusion of folk-noise, devotional singing,
and experimental auditory loomwork. By asking what ways can we explode the
erotic with a stranger, soundshapes strain toward language, a liturgical
failure-song: a place where undoing becomes testimony.

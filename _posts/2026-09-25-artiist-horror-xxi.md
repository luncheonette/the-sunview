After a three-month hiatus, ARTIST-HORROR returns! For our 21st edition, we
feature _Yours Truly, Jack the Ripper_, from 1961, in which the authorities
charged with apprehending the notorious serial killer discover that he is most
likely an artist, using the bohemian world of his colleagues as cover for his
horrible misdeeds. Also: he might be eternally young and still alive in the
1960s? Has he achieved Schiller's aesthetic ideal of "the extinction of time in
time and the reconciliation of becoming with absolute being"??? Maybe. Let's
see. Complete with beatniks and wildly supercilious art critics, and directed by
the legendary Ray Milland! Free pizza, free beer. Hangouts at 7, film at 8.

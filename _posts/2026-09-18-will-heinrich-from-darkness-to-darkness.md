_From Darkness To Darkness_ is a portrait of its hero, New York-based art critic Jonah Blank, through his intimate relationships. Handling bitter family arguments, uncomfortable studio visits, marital harmony and struggle and the passing sweetness of parenting with the same acute but delicate touch, Heinrich paints a moving picture of life—from birth to death—as something that can never quite be grasped.

“A masterclass in raw introspection, pitch-perfect dialogue, and precise
observation, From _Darkness To Darkness_ unfolds against the vivid backdrop of
contemporary New York City, and navigates the fraught space between the self and
those who shape it—especially family—as the narrator probes feelings of longing,
deep-seated friction, alienation, and the limits of self-expression.”<br>
―Mayada Ibrahim, Winner of the 2024 PEN Translates Award

“In From _Darkness To Darkness_, Will Heinrich has written a poignant New York
love story set against the relentless push and pull of work and family
obligations. While the negotiation to remain oneself must always continue,
Darkness speaks to the possibilities that can be found in dissatisfaction, the
humor of holding steady in the eye of a familial maelstrom, and the agony and
ecstasy of a never-ending search for beauty and truth.”<br>
―Julian Tepper, Cooler Heads

Bryce Hackford will play field rubbings; processed field recordings where the
source is in most instances fully removed, leaving only triggered spectral
traces to be heard.

Minor Characters play a muscle memory music. Three friends with shared
collective history in the subterranean spirit, they amassed in 2022 to play
consistent gigs in the Hudson Valley, where all 3 members are based. In other
realms; Tony Cenicola is the drummer of scuzz trippers Tono Bungay,and slammed
with Glenn Branca. Carter Thorton gleam’d on guitar with Enos Slaughter,
Izititis, and GNAW, and Matt Mottel co founded the confusion unit Talibam!

Duo featuring Warren Ng (Somnambulists) and Amye McCarther (Bleach Corals)
playing abstract, sun-damaged free-form guitar improvisations.

Come celebrate the launch of _Ghost Gravity: A Ballet Poem_ by Isabel Sobral
Campos, with a staged reading of an excerpt from the poem, featuring Anna
Gurton-Wachter, Stephon Lawrence, Leslie McIntosh, Isabel Sobral Campos, Kendra
Sullivan, and Mónica de la Torre. Ghost Gravity is structured as a series of
dance numbers in verse form, like a ballet, a work that unfolds through gesture,
repetition, and turn rather than narrative.

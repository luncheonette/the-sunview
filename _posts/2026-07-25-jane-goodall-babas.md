Sunview is one of the pickup spots for The 607 CSA, and this week some of that
community happens to be making music too.

Jane Goodall, a new trio featuring Andy Staszkiw (Andy's Guitar Repair), Charles
Burst (Seaside Lounge), and Tianna Kennedy, joins Brooklyn's own Babas for an
evening of songs.

Karna from Babas is a worker-owner at Brooklyn Packers, where CSA shares are
warehoused before heading out across the city, and Tianna helps run The 607 CSA
and the Catskills Agrarian Alliance, connecting regional farms with communities
throughout New York. We'll have information about both organizations at the show
if you're curious.
